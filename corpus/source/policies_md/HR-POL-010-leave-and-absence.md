# Leave and Absence Policy

**Company:** Promote Health Analytics Pty Ltd

## 1. Purpose

This policy provides a consistent process for personal leave, carer's leave, compassionate leave, unpaid leave, and other absences at Promote Health Analytics Pty Ltd. Paid planned annual leave remains governed by HR-POL-002.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to full-time and part-time employees. Eligibility for a particular leave category depends on the employee's circumstances, available records, and the applicable company rule.

## 3. Definitions

### 3.1 Personal leave

Leave used when the employee cannot work because of illness or injury.

### 3.2 Carer's leave

Leave used to provide care or support to an immediate family or household member.

### 3.3 Compassionate leave

Leave associated with a serious family or household event recognised by the company.

### 3.4 Unpaid leave

Approved leave for which no paid balance applies.

## 4. Policy Requirements

### 4.1 Accurate leave category

An absence must be recorded under the category that best reflects the circumstances.

Paid time off must not be used to obscure a personal, carer's, or other leave event where a different policy process applies.

### 4.2 Privacy and minimum information

The employee should provide enough information to support the request without disclosing unnecessary medical or personal detail.

Managers must handle absence information confidentially and involve People and Culture where sensitive information is required.

### 4.3 Evidence

People and Culture or the manager may request reasonable evidence where the duration, pattern, or operational impact warrants it.

Evidence must be stored only in the approved record.

### 4.4 Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave requires manager and People and Culture approval.

Approval depends on the reason, duration, operational impact, and any available paid leave option.

## 5. Procedures or Application

### 5.1 Planned absence

The employee should submit planned non-PTO leave as early as practicable and provide the expected dates and relevant category.

### 5.2 Unplanned absence notification

Personal and carer's leave requests should be reported as soon as practicable and no later than the employee's scheduled start time where possible.

If the employee is unable to notify the manager, another person may make initial contact, followed by the employee when practicable.

### 5.3 Extension or return

The employee must update the manager when the expected duration changes.

A return-to-work discussion may be arranged where support, safety, or work adjustments need consideration.

### 5.4 Escalation

Complex, extended, recurring, sensitive, or disputed absences must be referred to People and Culture.

The assistant must not make a medical judgement or determine the truth of sensitive personal circumstances.

### 5.5 Contact during absence

Contact should be proportionate to the expected duration, operational need, and employee circumstances. Managers should avoid repeated or intrusive contact and should agree on a reasonable update method where the absence continues.

An employee is not expected to perform ordinary work while recorded as unable to work. Necessary administrative communication should be brief and respectful.

### 5.6 Return-to-work support

A return discussion may address current capacity, temporary adjustments, priority work, communication needs, and any referral to People and Culture. The manager must not seek to diagnose the employee.

Where an adjustment or sensitive health matter is raised, People and Culture should coordinate the appropriate review and record handling.

### 5.7 Patterns and recurring absence

A recurring pattern may be reviewed where it creates operational concern or indicates that support may be needed. The review should use accurate records and should not assume wrongdoing.

Managers must consult People and Culture before taking formal action based on a sensitive or recurring absence pattern.

## 6. Exceptions and Escalation

- Emergency circumstances may prevent normal notification and evidence timeframes.
- Immediate personal safety takes priority over process timing.
- A request involving disability, medical adjustment, or sensitive health information must be escalated to People and Culture.

## 7. Responsibilities

### 7.1 Employees

Notify the manager, provide accurate dates, and maintain reasonable contact.

### 7.2 Managers

Record absences consistently, protect privacy, and escalate complex cases.

### 7.3 People and Culture

Provide policy interpretation and manage sensitive or extended cases.

## 8. Decision Rules

- If the request is planned paid annual leave, use HR-POL-002.
- If the employee reports an unplanned personal or carer's absence, record notification as soon as practicable and avoid requesting unnecessary detail.
- If the request is unpaid leave, obtain manager and People and Culture approval.
- If the matter includes sensitive medical information, escalate rather than adjudicate.

## 9. Examples

### 9.1 Unplanned illness

An employee becomes unwell before work and notifies the manager before the scheduled start time without providing unnecessary clinical detail.

### 9.2 Insufficient PTO balance

An employee seeking additional time away may discuss unpaid leave, but approval is not automatic.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

### 10.1 Do I need to describe my diagnosis?

Not ordinarily. Provide sufficient information for the leave process and discuss sensitive requirements with People and Culture.

### 10.2 Can I use PTO instead of personal leave?

Use the category that accurately reflects the circumstances.

### 10.3 Who approves unpaid leave?

The manager and People and Culture.
