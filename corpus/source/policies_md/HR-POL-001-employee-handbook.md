# Employee Handbook

**Company:** Promote Health Analytics Pty Ltd

## 1. Purpose

This handbook introduces the working principles, policy framework, and support pathways used by Promote Health Analytics Pty Ltd. It helps employees locate authoritative policies and understand how responsibilities are shared across employees, managers, People and Culture, Information Security, and Finance.

## 2. Scope

This handbook applies to employees, contractors, managers, and leaders. It is a navigational and cultural document. It does not replace the detailed requirements in the authoritative policies listed in the Related Documents section.

## 3. Definitions

### 3.1 Authoritative policy

The policy document that owns a specific entitlement, threshold, approval rule, exception, or escalation requirement.

### 3.2 People and Culture

The internal function responsible for employment support, policy administration, and HR case management.

### 3.3 Manager

The employee's recorded line manager or an authorised delegate.

### 3.4 Company systems

Applications, information assets, devices, and services approved for company work.

## 4. Policy Requirements

### 4.1 Policy-first decision making

Employees should consult the relevant authoritative policy before relying on a summary, informal message, or prior practice.

Managers must apply policy consistently and must escalate uncertainty rather than creating local exceptions.

Policy summaries in this handbook are orientation aids only and must not be used as the sole source for a detailed entitlement or approval decision.

### 4.2 Respectful and secure work

All workers must contribute to a respectful, inclusive, and safe workplace.

All workers must protect confidential information and use company systems only for authorised purposes.

Concerns involving harassment, discrimination, retaliation, threats, or significant security risk must be escalated through the appropriate formal channel.

### 4.3 Employee responsibilities

Employees must keep their contact, manager, and work-location information current.

Employees must provide accurate information when requesting leave, remote work, reimbursement, benefits support, equipment, or HR assistance.

Employees must obtain required approval before committing the company to travel, expense, or operational obligations.

## 5. Procedures or Application

### 5.1 Finding the right policy

Use HR-POL-002 for paid time off and planned annual leave.

Use HR-POL-004 for remote and flexible work, including overseas arrangements.

Use HR-POL-005 for information-security and acceptable-use requirements.

Use HR-POL-013 when a request requires an HR ticket, documented escalation, or a mock action.

### 5.2 Requesting help

An employee should first provide the relevant employee ID, request type, requested dates or location, and any immediate deadline.

The system may retrieve policy evidence and employee-specific mock data before presenting a response.

Where the evidence is incomplete, the employee should contact People and Culture instead of relying on an unsupported conclusion.

### 5.3 Policy navigation and interpretation

Employees should begin with the policy whose title most closely matches the request. A paid-time-off question belongs in the Paid Time Off Policy, while an unplanned health-related absence belongs in the Leave and Absence Policy. Where a request spans more than one policy, the response should identify the role of each document instead of blending their rules into one unsupported summary.

A policy title, section heading, and supporting passage should accompany each material policy claim. This approach allows the employee to distinguish an authoritative rule from general guidance, an example, or a suggested next step. A citation must not be attached to a claim that the cited passage does not actually support.

### 5.4 Records and policy changes

Employees should rely on the current active policy version. Drafts, superseded documents, informal team notes, and historical practices do not override an active policy. People and Culture coordinates changes and communicates material updates through approved channels.

Questions created by a policy change should be resolved using the effective date, the date of the employee event, and any documented transition arrangement. The handbook does not create a transition rule on its own.

## 6. Exceptions and Escalation

- This handbook does not create an entitlement, financial threshold, leave balance, or approval decision.
- Where a summary conflicts with an authoritative policy, the authoritative policy governs.
- Sensitive workplace matters must be escalated and must not be decided solely through automated guidance.

## 7. Responsibilities

### 7.1 Employees

Read applicable policies, provide accurate information, and obtain approval before acting.

### 7.2 Managers

Apply policy consistently, document decisions, and escalate exceptions.

### 7.3 People and Culture

Maintain the policy framework and manage employment-related escalations.

### 7.4 Information Security

Maintain security controls and review elevated information risks.

## 8. Decision Rules

- If a question asks for a detailed entitlement or threshold, retrieve the authoritative policy rather than relying on the handbook.
- If the available policy evidence is incomplete or conflicting, escalate to People and Culture.
- If a request concerns a sensitive workplace issue, retrieve the conduct policy and escalate without adjudicating the matter.

## 9. Examples

### 9.1 Finding PTO guidance

An employee asking about three days of planned leave is directed to HR-POL-002 rather than receiving an entitlement from this handbook.

### 9.2 Finding remote-work guidance

An employee proposing overseas work is directed to HR-POL-004 and HR-POL-005 because both approval and security requirements apply.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

### 10.1 Is this handbook the final source for every HR rule?

No. It explains the policy framework and directs employees to the authoritative document.

### 10.2 Where should I ask for a policy exception?

Contact People and Culture and provide the relevant facts, policy reference, and business reason.

### 10.3 Can the assistant decide a harassment complaint?

No. It must retrieve the conduct policy and escalate the matter.
