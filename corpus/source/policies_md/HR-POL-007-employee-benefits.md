# Employee Benefits Policy

**Company:** Promote Health Analytics Pty Ltd

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines eligibility, commencement, election, and administration of the company-sponsored benefit programs offered by Promote Health Analytics Pty Ltd.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to full-time and part-time employees. Contractors are not eligible unless a written contract expressly provides a named benefit.

## 3. Definitions

### 3.1 Eligible employee

A full-time or part-time employee who satisfies the coverage-commencement rule.

### 3.2 Election

An employee's recorded choice to participate in an available benefit.

### 3.3 Coverage start

The date on which an approved benefit becomes active.

## 4. Policy Requirements

### 4.1 Eligibility by employment type

Full-time and part-time employees are eligible for company benefits.

Contractors are not eligible unless a written contract states otherwise.

Probation does not remove eligibility, but coverage begins under the commencement rule.

### 4.2 Coverage commencement

Benefits coverage begins on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment.

People and Culture must record the coverage-start date consistently with the employee's start date.

### 4.3 Available programs

Available programs may include health support, wellbeing services, professional development, and other company-approved benefits.

A benefit description does not create an entitlement beyond the recorded eligibility and election.

### 4.4 Employee elections

An employee must complete required election information within the communicated enrolment period.

An incomplete election remains pending until the required information is provided.

## 5. Procedures or Application

### 5.1 Eligibility check

People and Culture confirms employment type, start date, and any contract-specific exception.

The benefits record must state eligible, ineligible, or pending.

### 5.2 Election changes

An employee may request a change during an approved enrolment period or after an eligible life or employment event recognised by the program.

The employee must provide the required information before the change becomes effective.

### 5.3 Leaving employment

People and Culture confirms the end of company-sponsored coverage and provides relevant transition information.

### 5.4 Benefits information and privacy

Benefits records should contain only information required to determine eligibility, administer an election, or support the employee. Sensitive information must be handled through approved systems and must not be placed in ordinary chat or ticket summaries.

Managers should not ask employees to disclose detailed health or family information. People and Culture coordinates information required by a benefit administrator.

### 5.5 Record correction

An employee who believes the eligibility, election, or coverage-start record is incorrect should identify the affected benefit and provide the relevant employment or election information. People and Culture reviews the source record and documents any correction.

A correction may change a future or current status but does not create coverage for a period that the program does not support.

### 5.6 Program changes

The company may add, change, or discontinue a discretionary benefit after appropriate review and communication. A program change should identify the effective date and any transition arrangement.

An employee should not rely on an informal statement or prior-year offering when making a current benefits decision.

## 6. Exceptions and Escalation

- A contractor receives only a benefit expressly named in a written contract.
- A delayed or incomplete election may postpone coverage even where the employee is otherwise eligible.
- Benefit-provider terms may affect available services, but they do not change the employment-type eligibility rule in this policy.

## 7. Responsibilities

### 7.1 Employees

Provide accurate election information and notify People and Culture of relevant changes.

### 7.2 People and Culture

Determine eligibility, record coverage dates, and administer elections.

### 7.3 Managers

Direct employees to People and Culture and avoid promising unrecorded benefits.

## 8. Decision Rules

- If employment_type is full_time or part_time, the employee is eligible subject to the coverage-start date.
- If employment_type is contractor, benefits are ineligible unless a written contract names an exception.
- If required election information is incomplete, status remains pending.

## 9. Examples

### 9.1 Full-time new starter

A full-time employee starts on 10 January. Coverage begins on 1 March, the first day of the month following 30 days of employment.

### 9.2 Contractor

A contractor without a specific benefit clause is recorded as ineligible.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

### 10.1 Am I eligible while on probation?

Yes, if you are a full-time or part-time employee, but coverage starts under the 30-day commencement rule.

### 10.2 Are contractors eligible?

Not unless the written contract expressly provides a named benefit.

### 10.3 Why is my status pending?

Required election information or the coverage-start condition may not yet be complete.
